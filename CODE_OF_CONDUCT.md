# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We are committed to a respectful, constructive environment for contributors and users.
Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully
- focus on technical facts and actionable feedback
- assume good intent while holding a high quality bar

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks,
or publishing private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code of conduct.
They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and
other contributions that violate these standards.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, open a private maintainer contact via repository maintainers
or a private security/advisory channel when appropriate.
